<objective>
Deliver DIST-01 as a fully-testable, end-to-end vertical slice: a member of the GSD
community can switch the interface language between pt-BR and English, the choice
persists to appDataDir, and it is restored (enum-validated) on the next boot — locked
against future regression by an automated 9-namespace key-parity test.

## Phase Goal (user story — derived from ROADMAP Goal + Success Criterion 1)

**As a** member of the GSD community, **I want to** switch the interface between
Portuguese (pt-BR) and English and have my choice remembered, **so that** I can use
GSD Cards in my own language every time I open it.

Purpose: This is the phase TRACER. It proves the i18n + persistence + boot-restore
seam (the one genuinely stateful path in Phase 5) on the agent's best early-context
tokens, before the updater/bundler/CI plans build on the same app-store and AppShell
mount surfaces.
Output: `LanguageSwitcher` component, `app-store` language persistence with closed-enum
validation, an AppShell boot-restore effect, both mount points, and the parity guard.
